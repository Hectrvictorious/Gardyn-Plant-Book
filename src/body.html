How these maps work
  • Light zones (Gardyn's): high is the 12-pod center cluster: the center column in rows 3–8, plus the outer pods that point toward the center. Medium is rows 2 and 9 plus the outer pods that point away. Low is rows 1 and 10. Gardyn's fallback is one zone lower when the ideal seat is full, and its own kit layouts put medium plants in high seats.
  • Facing: odd rows point right ►, even rows point left ◄. The pod at the arrow end leans into open air, so the row's biggest plant gets it.
  • Headroom: the pod directly above faces the other way, so a plant's real competition is the pod two rows up on its own side. Giants ▲ sit in rows 1–2, or under a Cap two rows up.
  • Pairs: an A/B pair shares a row or sits on a diagonal, never two rows apart in one column, where B would grow straight into A.
  • Salt rule (fruiting): nothing salt-sensitive sits below a tomato in the same Column while that tomato stands. That's what the beans taught.
  • Vines climb into light, bushes lean off: tomatoes down the light bars, cucamelon up the Trellis, ground cherry off the left side.
  • Window: assumed on the right of the salad tower; the light-hungry outer plants sit on that side.
  • Nothing moves until you say so. "Now" is where plants sat on Oct 27, updated by the moves you tick off. "Nov 2 plan" is the re-seating by light and space.

About this app

Built for Josh's two Gardyn Home v3 towers on Swan's Island. Plant facts paraphrase Gardyn's Plant Book (its Help Center plant articles) and, for plants Gardyn doesn't carry, extension and seed-company sources linked on each page. Seats come from your maps: salad v4 and v5, fruiting v8 and v9.

Not affiliated with or endorsed by Gardyn. Gardyn, Kelby, yCube, yPod, Plant Food and HydroBoost are Gardyn's names.
